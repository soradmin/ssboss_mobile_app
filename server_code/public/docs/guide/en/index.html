Buyers

Instructions for buyers

Use SSBOSS via the mobile app (iOS/Android) or ssboss.shop. One account works on both.

Getting started

Buyer

Download SSBOSS from the App Store or Google Play. Browse products on the home feed.

Create an account in Profile → Sign up. Confirm your email. Use the bottom nav: Home, Catalog, Cart, Favorites, Profile.

Catalog & search

Open Catalog, pick a category. Tap the heart to save, cart icon to add, or the card to view details.

Cart & orders

Add items to cart, checkout with delivery/pickup and payment method. Track orders in Profile → My orders.

Shopping on the website

Browse ssboss.shop, open a product page, add to cart or buy now. Same account as the app.

Sellers

Instructions for sellers

Manage your store via the SSBOSS admin panel: products, orders, and settings.

Order processing

Seller

Go to Orders in the admin panel. View customer details, items, payment and delivery status. Update status and print invoice when ready.

Become a seller

  • Click "Become a seller" on the website or contact SSBOSS support
  • After approval, access the admin panel
  • Add products under Products, use bulk upload for large catalogs
